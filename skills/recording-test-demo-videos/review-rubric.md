# Review rubric – Recording test demo videos

Shared rules (scoring, gate, output, process criteria P1–P6): [../review-rubric-shared.md](../review-rubric-shared.md).
Source: [BRIEF] – the project's video requirements.

## V1 – Requirements

| # | M/S | Criterion | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | M | Every video requirement of the brief is a checkbox with its source | Count of requirement sentences = rows; each names its video |

## V2 – Shot list

| # | M/S | Criterion | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | M | Every requirement is covered by at least one scene | The "Scenes" column of the requirements table names a scene for every V1 row; each video has its own scene table |
| 3 | M | Identity proof comes first and is live | Scene 1 = `whoami` / `hostname` or face-cam |
| 4 | M | The fix scene shows a real fix | Names the commit and the review row it comes from |
| 5 | S | Narration points are bullets in the narration language | No word-for-word script unless the tester asked |
| 6 | S | The planned length reaches the minimum | Sum of durations ≥ minimum per video |

## V3–V4 – Prepare and record

| # | M/S | Criterion | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | M | Recording and voice are the tester's | The agent produced no audio, no synthetic voice, no recording |
| 8 | S | Preparation checklist given before recording | Private apps, data reset, fonts, microphone, dry run |

## V5 – Cut and check

| # | M/S | Criterion | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | M | Every requirement has a timestamp in the final cut | Check table complete, timestamps from the tester |
| 10 | M | Runs and reports shown are real and uncut where it matters | Command + output in one shot; report title shows identity + timestamp |
| 11 | M | Links work and are where the project says | Opened in a private window; README / report updated |
